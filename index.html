Edward J. Schwartz

Introduction

Hi!

I'm a Ph.D. student in the Electronic and Computer Engineering Department at Carnegie Mellon University. I am interested in almost all aspects of Computer Security. Currently, I am most interested in binary program analysis, verification conditions, and operating system defenses.

I've graduated, and now work for the malware analysis team at CERT as a research scientist.
